Assets:

  • Characters (slightly modified)

How to play:

  • Both players start with the same stats (50 hp, 15 attack, 25% dodge chance)
  • Each player has 4 actions:
    • Attack - deal your attack damage to the other player
    • Heal - You have a 50% chance to heal between 15-20 hp
    • Lower Attack / Dodge - You can lower the stats of the other player
